# Business_Analytics_Bootcamp_1

The Case study briefing used in Introduction to A/B Testing. The subject for our study is Krushing Kingdoms By DataHero Entertainment, which was widely loved for multiplayer online role-playing game.

==> Objective:
      Evaluate the effect of different team level access points (level 5 vs. level 7) on player engagement and retention.
      
==> Dataset Structure:
      ●	id: Unique identifier for each player.
      ●	team_level_access: Indicates the experimental group to which the player belongs ('level_5' or 'level_7').
      ●	minutes_played: Total minutes played by the player.
      ●	day1_retention: Boolean indicating whether the player was active on the first day after joining (TRUE for active, FALSE for inactive).
      ●	day7_retention: Boolean indicating whether the player was active on the seventh day after joining (TRUE for active, FALSE for inactive).

      
  ==> Potential Insights:
          The analysis will provide insights into how different access levels to teams influence player behavior in terms of game engagement and retention. This can guide future game design decisions,       
          particularly in relation to team dynamics and access criteria.




# Introduction to A/B Testing

--> A/B Testing is also known as split testing, is an experimental technique used to compare two versions of something(e.g., a website, feature, product, or marketing strategy) to determine which performs better.

# Randomization Technique for A/B Testing

--> Randomization ensures that the differences observed between the control and treatment groups are attributable to the changes made in the experimental(the independent variable) and not due to pre-existing differences between the groups or other external factors. 

--> The different techniques for Randomization are:

* Simple Randomization

* Block Randomization

* Stratified Randomization

* Cluster Randomization
